## Context

The robotics club is electing officers for the year. I am the only candidate for
president; the treasurer role is uncontested and much lighter. I want the visibility
and the leadership practice, but I am taking a heavy course load this term.

## Options

**Club president**
- Runs weekly meetings, organizes 3-4 events per term
- 6-8 hours per week during term
- Owns the club's budget and event sign-off
- Strongest resume signal

**Club treasurer**
- Tracks membership dues and expenses
- 1-2 hours per week
- No meeting leadership
- Weaker resume signal

## Decision

Chose president. The resume line and the leadership experience outweighed the time cost
in my estimation, and I planned to run the meetings efficiently.

## Outcome

Reviewed 2026-06-30. Result: worse.

The time assumption was the wrong one. I did not have enough free time each week to
lead the meetings and run the events, and the exam-period collision was exactly as bad
as I feared. I missed two weekly meetings and handed event planning to a first-year who
did a decent job but had no context.

The second failed assumption was that the other members would handle their assigned
tasks without chasing. Every task needed three follow-ups. I spent more time
chasing than doing, and the two events I owned were thin because I could not sustain
the schedule. The resume line is real and did land on my CV, but the underlying
experience was mostly administrative firefighting, which is not the thing I wanted to
show. Next time I would take the lighter role and ask for a project rather than a title.

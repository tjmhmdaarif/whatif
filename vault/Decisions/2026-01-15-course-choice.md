## Context

Choosing between a 12-week in-person data bootcamp (around 4,000 dollars, full-time
commitment, 2h daily commute) and a self-paced online course (around 400 dollars, part-time,
no deadlines). I am in my third year and need to decide before the spring registration window.

The bootcamp advertises a job placement rate and an alumni network. The online course
covers the same material and has a certification but no cohort.

## Options

**In-person bootcamp**
- 12 weeks, full time, 4,000 USD
- Weekly on-site lectures and lab sessions
- Career services and an alumni network
- Two-hour commute each way

**Self-paced online course**
- 40 hours of material, no fixed schedule
- 400 USD
- Certificate of completion only
- Flexible around my existing coursework

## Decision

Chose the in-person bootcamp. The career support and structure felt worth the cost and
the time, and I believed the network would carry the value.

## Outcome

Reviewed 2026-07-20. Result: worse.

The cohort was mostly people changing careers, not students, so the "network" was a
separate social world I did not fit into. Two classmates quit in week three, and the
group chat went quiet after week five. I lost about 25 hours a week and two grades
dropped because of the commute and the workload. I did land one referral, but it came
from a lecture assistant rather than the alumni network, and the placement numbers I had
assumed were accurate turned out to count anyone who attended a single employer talk.
Finishing it was worth something, but not what I paid for it.
